What Documents Should a Chinese Supplier Provide Before an Order?

Learn what documents a Chinese supplier may provide before samples or orders, including business license, invoice details, product specs, certificates, packing information, and sample terms.

Not a formal factory audit. Not legal due diligence. Not quality inspection. Not a supplier reliability guarantee.

Short answer

The point is not to collect documents for comfort. It is to collect the few documents that make the supplier’s claims easier to test.

Why this matters

Missing documents often mean missing clarity around what exactly is being quoted, sampled, packed, and paid for.

Step-by-step checklist

Outline placeholder: business license, quotation/PI details, product specification sheet, packing list details, sample terms, and certificate scope.

Common red flags

Outline placeholder: documents with mismatched names, incomplete scope, product mismatch, and refusal to confirm written versions.

Example supplier questions

Outline placeholder: questions about business name, PI details, product model match, packing scope, and sample conditions.

What this cannot prove

Documents alone cannot prove product quality, business integrity, or future order execution.

Boundary

This guide helps identify practical documents, not formal audit, legal due diligence, quality inspection, or supplier reliability guarantee.
