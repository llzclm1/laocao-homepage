Quotation language

Chinese Supplier Says “Best Price”: What Should a Buyer Ask?

“This is our best price” sounds final, but it usually answers only one part of a buying decision. Before comparing the number or moving to a sample, make the product, quantity, quotation scope, and payment assumptions visible.

This is communication guidance for buyers. It is not supplier verification, a factory audit, legal due diligence, quality inspection, payment safety advice, or a guarantee of an order outcome.

Prepared from recurring supplier communication patterns. Examples are generalized and do not identify a customer or supplier.

Direct answer

Ask what “best” is based on. Request the exact product version, quantity range, material, packaging, shipping basis, payment terms, and price validity. Until those conditions are written down, a best-price statement is not a like-for-like quotation.

Price statement, not scope statement

The supplier has provided a price position. The buyer still needs to know what the number includes and which conditions could change it.

What the phrase confirms—and leaves open

Read the short reply as a communication signal, then ask for the missing basis.

Supplier saysWhat it confirmsWhat is still unclear
“This is our best price.”The supplier has stated a price.Quantity basis, product version, included items, packaging, shipping, and validity.
“No more discount.”The supplier is setting a negotiation position.Whether the scope can change, which costs are optional, and whether a different quantity changes the price.
“Price is for standard quality.”The supplier has named a broad quality label.Material grade, finish, tolerance, test requirements, and the reference sample or specification.

Build a usable price basis

Product: Record the model, drawing, dimensions, material, finish, and customization.

Quantity: Ask for the price at the quantity you may actually order and any price breaks above or below it.

Scope: Separate product, packaging, labels, tooling, testing, and accessories. Mark each item included, excluded, or optional.

Shipping: State the Incoterm, destination, shipping method, and whether freight is inside or outside the quoted number.

Payment: Record currency, deposit, balance, payment recipient, and what event starts production.

Validity: Ask how long the price applies and what changes would trigger a revised quote.

Questions to send back

Could you confirm what exact product version this price is based on?

What quantity range and price tiers does this quote use?

Which material, finish, packaging, labels, and accessories are included?

Are tooling, testing, shipping, and sample costs included or separate?

What payment terms and beneficiary details apply?

How long is the price valid, and which changes would require a new quotation?

Keep the comparison fair

Ask every supplier the same questions. A shorter answer is not necessarily a lower price; it may simply describe less scope.

Supplier message

“This is our best price: $6.90 per piece.”

FAQ

What does this is our best price mean from a Chinese supplier?

It means the supplier has stated a price position. It does not explain the quantity basis, product version, included costs, packaging, payment terms, or how long the price applies.

How should I respond when a supplier says best price?

Ask the supplier to confirm the exact product and quantity basis, what is included, packaging and shipping assumptions, payment terms, and the conditions that could change the price.

Should I compare a best-price quote with another supplier quote?

Only after the product, quantity, material, packaging, shipping, payment, and sample assumptions are aligned. A lower number may describe a narrower scope.

Does a best-price statement prove the supplier is reliable?

No. It is a price statement, not proof of reliability, quality, production capacity, or future delivery.
