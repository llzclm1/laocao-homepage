How to Compare Chinese Supplier Quotations Beyond Price

Learn how to compare Chinese supplier quotations beyond price, including MOQ, material, packaging, sample terms, lead time, payment terms, customization, and unclear assumptions.

Not a formal factory audit. Not legal due diligence. Not quality inspection. Not a supplier reliability guarantee.

Short answer

A cheaper quote is often just a quote with more hidden assumptions. Compare the assumptions first, then compare the price.

Why this matters

Buyers lose time when they compare neat-looking numbers that are actually describing different products, packaging scopes, or sample paths.

Step-by-step checklist

Outline placeholder: product version, material, MOQ, lead time, packaging, sample terms, payment terms, and shipping assumptions.

Common red flags

Outline placeholder: very low price with vague scope, missing packaging, unclear sample logic, and no statement of what is excluded.

Example supplier questions

Outline placeholder: direct clarification questions to force each supplier to quote against the same written basis.
