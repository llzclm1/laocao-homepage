How to Compare Chinese Supplier Quotations Beyond Price

Learn how to compare Chinese supplier quotations beyond price, including MOQ, material, packaging, sample terms, lead time, payment terms, customization, and unclear assumptions.

Not a formal factory audit. Not legal due diligence. Not quality inspection. Not a supplier reliability guarantee.

Short answer

Compare the assumptions first. A low price is not useful if each supplier is quoting a different product, packaging scope, or sample basis.

Prepared by Lao Cao from recurring supplier communication patterns. Examples are generalized and do not identify a customer or supplier.

Why this matters

Quote comparison becomes misleading when buyers line up numbers without lining up context. One supplier may quote stock material, another may assume custom material. One may include simple packaging, another may not. One may treat the sample as reference only, another as a custom pre-production sample. Without a common written basis, price comparison becomes noise.

If you are still comparing overall supplier fit and communication before narrowing the list, use the supplier comparison framework first.

Step-by-step checklist

1. Define one written RFQ basis before collecting quotes.

2. Compare product version, material, packaging, and customization scope.

3. Compare MOQ, lead time, and sample path assumptions.

4. Compare payment terms and invoicing entity.

5. Compare what each supplier still needs before the quote becomes firm.

Common red flags

A very low quote with weak detail is one red flag. Another is when one supplier answers quickly but never states what is excluded. If the supplier cannot define the product version, packaging, or sample basis clearly, the price itself is not ready to compare.

Example supplier questions

Can you confirm what exact product version this quote is based on?

Can you confirm what packaging and labeling are included?

Can you confirm whether the sample is stock, similar, or custom?

What details are still open before this price becomes firm?

What this cannot prove

This cannot prove actual factory quality, future delivery, or long-term supplier reliability. It only helps compare quotation clarity.
