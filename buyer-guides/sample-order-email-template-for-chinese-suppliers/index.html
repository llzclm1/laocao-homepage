Buyer Guide / Sample Email

Sample Order Email Template for Chinese Suppliers

Use a focused sample request to confirm what the sample represents, which specifications are included, how much it costs, when it can ship, and what may change before bulk production.

A sample request clarifies communication. It does not verify the supplier or guarantee that future production will match the sample.

Direct Answer

A sample order email should identify the product, sample type, specification, customization, cost, production time, shipping method, and the questions the supplier must answer before payment.

Define what the sample represents

Ask whether it is a stock sample, similar reference, customized sample, or pre-production sample.

Sample Request Email Template

Adjust the template to the actual product.

Subject: Sample request for [product name]

Hello [supplier name],

We would like to review a sample of [product name] before considering a larger order.

Please confirm:

1. Whether this is a stock, reference, customized, or pre-production sample
2. Material, dimensions, color, finish, function, and packaging included
3. Sample price and shipping cost
4. Any tooling, printing, or customization fee
5. Sample preparation time and expected dispatch date
6. Shipping method and tracking availability
7. What may change before bulk production
8. Whether any sample fee is deductible from a future order

Please also confirm the payment recipient, currency, and invoice details before payment.

Thank you.

Information to Attach

Product reference

  • Product name or model
  • Photos, drawings, links, or reference files
  • Intended use or target market where relevant

Required specifications

  • Material and dimensions
  • Color, finish, logo, label, and function
  • Details that cannot be substituted

Customization request

  • Files or artwork available
  • Product and packaging changes
  • Questions about tooling or setup

Order context

  • Expected future quantity
  • Target date
  • Destination country
  • Any packaging or shipping constraints

Questions to Review in the Reply

  • Did the supplier answer the actual sample type?A generic “sample available” reply does not explain what will be sent.
  • Are specifications confirmed?Separate confirmed details from assumptions and alternatives.
  • Are all fees visible?Check sample, customization, tooling, packaging, and shipping costs.
  • Are production and delivery time separate?Sample preparation happens before carrier delivery.
  • Is the bulk connection clear?Ask what will stay the same and what may change later.

Key Takeaway

The email should help the buyer understand what will be received before paying. A useful supplier reply connects the requested specifications, sample terms, timeline, shipping, and possible bulk-production changes.
