Factory Signals

How to Check If a Chinese Factory Is Real

A practical checklist for overseas buyers to check whether a Chinese supplier may be a real factory, including workshop evidence, product control, business license, video call, and reply consistency.

Not a formal factory audit. Not legal due diligence. Not quality inspection. Not a supplier reliability guarantee.

Short answer

Look for business consistency, workshop evidence tied to your product type, and reply quality that shows real process familiarity.

Why this matters

A real company may still be the wrong fit, and a polished profile can still hide weak product control or weak communication.

Step-by-step checklist

Outline placeholder: business license, workshop views, product-category match, video call requests, packing evidence, and technical reply checks.

Common red flags

Outline placeholder: generic workshop photos, mixed product lines, evasive video calls, inconsistent business details, and vague technical answers.

Example supplier questions

Outline placeholder: factory walkthrough questions, line/process questions, product-specific evidence requests, and account/company match questions.

What this cannot prove

This cannot prove legal standing, production reliability over time, on-site quality control, or future order outcome.

Boundary

This guide helps read visible evidence, not formal audit, legal due diligence, quality inspection, or supplier reliability guarantee.
