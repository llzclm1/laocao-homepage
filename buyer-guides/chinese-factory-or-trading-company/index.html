Factory vs Trading

Is Your Chinese Supplier a Factory or Trading Company?

Learn practical ways to check whether a Chinese supplier may be a factory or trading company, including product range, workshop evidence, technical replies, and business consistency.

Not a formal factory audit. Not legal due diligence. Not quality inspection. Not a supplier reliability guarantee.

Short answer

The core question is not “factory or trading company” by itself. It is whether the supplier is transparent about what they actually control.

Why this matters

Buyers often treat “factory” as automatically safer, even when the real issue is process transparency and communication quality.
