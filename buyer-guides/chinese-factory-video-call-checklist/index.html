Chinese Factory Video Call Checklist for Overseas Buyers

Use this Chinese factory video call checklist to ask for workshop, sample room, packaging area, labels, production flow, and product-specific evidence before placing an order.

Not a formal factory audit. Not legal due diligence. Not quality inspection. Not a supplier reliability guarantee.

Short answer

A useful video call shows process, product relevance, and continuity, not just a quick camera sweep of a clean room.

Why this matters

Video calls can reduce ambiguity, but only if the buyer asks for scenes that connect to the real product flow.

Step-by-step checklist

Outline placeholder: workshop route, sample room, current production, packaging area, labels, raw material handling, and product-specific proof.

Common red flags

Outline placeholder: fixed-angle calls, refusal to show flow, generic rooms, missing packaging views, and answers that do not match what the camera shows.

Example supplier questions

Outline placeholder: questions about line flow, current SKU, packaging process, labels, and what part is in-house vs outsourced.

What this cannot prove

This cannot prove legal compliance, sustained capacity, product quality, or full production control.

Boundary

This guide helps structure a practical video call, not formal audit, legal due diligence, quality inspection, or supplier reliability guarantee.
