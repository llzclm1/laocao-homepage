MOQ and quotation changes

Chinese Supplier Changed the MOQ After Quotation: What Should a Buyer Ask?

A changed minimum order quantity can make an earlier quote difficult to compare. Before assuming a problem, put the original and updated product, packaging, customization, and production conditions side by side.

This is communication guidance for buyers. It is not supplier verification, a factory audit, legal due diligence, quality inspection, payment safety advice, or a guarantee of an order outcome.

Prepared from recurring supplier communication patterns. Examples are generalized and do not identify a customer or supplier.

Direct answer

Ask what changed between the first quotation and the new MOQ. A higher or lower number may follow a different product version, material, packaging, tooling, or production condition. Do not compare the numbers until the scope is aligned.

Changed condition, not a verdict

The updated MOQ confirms that the supplier has provided a new quantity condition. It does not, by itself, explain why the condition changed.

Read the MOQ change as a communication pattern

Separate what the supplier stated from the conditions that still need a direct answer.

Supplier saysWhat it confirmsWhat is still unclear
“The MOQ is different now.”The quantity condition has been updated.Reason for the change, product scope, material, packaging, and production assumptions.
“For custom products, MOQ is 1,000 pieces.”A custom-product quantity condition was provided.What makes the product custom, whether tooling or setup is included, and whether a stock version has a different MOQ.
“The original MOQ was for standard packaging.”The supplier identified a packaging assumption.Which packaging options change the MOQ, price, lead time, or sample basis.

Compare the original and updated basis

Product. Match model, drawing, dimensions, material, finish, and logo or customization.

Quantity. Record the original MOQ, new MOQ, price tiers, and whether the unit price changed with the quantity.

Packaging. Note carton, labels, inserts, printing, and whether packaging is standard or custom.

Production. Ask whether tooling, setup, material purchasing, or a separate production line affects the minimum.

Timing. Check whether the new MOQ changes sample timing, production lead time, or shipping assumptions.

Quote status. Ask which quotation replaces the earlier one and what terms remain valid.

Questions to send back

What changed between the original and current MOQ?

Does the new MOQ apply to the same product version, material, finish, and packaging?

Is tooling, setup, artwork, or a custom material assumption included?

What price tiers and lead times apply at the original and new quantities?

Which quotation should we use as the current written basis?

Can you confirm what remains unchanged from the earlier quote?

Ask for the delta

A useful reply identifies the exact changed line, not just a new total quantity.

Anonymous example: MOQ changes after a quote

Generic example only. It does not refer to a real buyer, supplier, or order.

Original message

“MOQ 500 pieces. Standard carton. $8.40 each.”


Updated message

“MOQ is 1,000 pieces now. Custom packaging required.”


What is confirmed

The supplier provided an updated minimum and linked it to custom packaging.


What is missing

The packaging specification, setup or printing cost, price at 500 pieces, and whether the product scope is otherwise unchanged are not stated.


Next question

Could you confirm the packaging change, any setup cost, the price and lead time at both quantities, and which quotation terms remain unchanged?

FAQ

Why would a Chinese supplier change the MOQ after a quotation?

The product version, material, packaging, customization, tooling, production method, or quantity assumptions may have changed. Ask the supplier to identify the exact condition rather than guessing.

What should I ask when the MOQ changes?

Ask what changed, which product and packaging scope the new MOQ covers, whether tooling or setup is involved, and whether the quotation and price tiers must be revised.

Can I compare the original and new MOQ directly?

Only after both MOQs are tied to the same product version, material, packaging, customization, and production assumptions.

Does a changed MOQ prove that a supplier is unreliable?

No. It shows that a quantity condition changed or was clarified. The buyer needs a written explanation and a comparable quotation basis.

Need to compare the two replies?

Send the original and updated quotation or supplier message when the changed MOQ leaves the next decision unclear.
