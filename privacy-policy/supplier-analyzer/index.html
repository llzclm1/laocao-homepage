Supplier Analyzer Privacy Policy

This policy explains what the Supplier Analyzer Chrome extension reads from a public page, how it displays that information, and where it stores it.

Effective date: September 20, 2026. This policy covers the extension and does not replace the separate GEWUJI website privacy policy.

In brief
  • Reading starts only after your toolbar action.
  • Captured records stay in your browser.
  • No page data is sent to a Supplier Analyzer server.

What This Covers

This policy applies to Supplier Analyzer, a Chrome extension that organizes supplier information already visible on the current public page into a local evidence record and follow-up questions.

The extension is designed for public supplier, company, and product pages. It is not a supplier verification, factory audit, or guarantee service.

Information the Extension Reads

Supplier Analyzer reads the current page only after you choose its toolbar action and grant the temporary access provided by Chrome.

  • Page contextThe page URL, page title, and canonical link when present.
  • Visible fieldsCompany name, location, website, business type, products, founding year, employee count, certifications, contact details, MOQ, and lead time when those values are visible on the page.
  • Source evidenceShort source excerpts, source type, capture time, and DOM or JSON-LD locator information used to show where a value came from.
  • Page structureRelevant visible text and structured JSON-LD data needed to identify the fields. Invalid or unsupported data may be recorded as missing or requiring clarification.

The extension does not ask for or intentionally collect passwords, cookies, payment information, browsing history, or content from other tabs. Do not use it on pages containing information you do not want stored in this browser profile.

How Information Is Used

  • OrganizeBuild a local record from fields already visible on the page.
  • ExplainShow evidence, source type, and a DOM or JSON-LD locator beside each captured value.
  • ClarifyPlace missing, conflicting, duplicate, relative-link, or otherwise uncertain values into follow-up questions.
  • RestoreLet you reopen saved records in the side panel for your own review.

Supplier Analyzer does not contact a supplier, send a message, place an order, make a purchasing decision, or claim that a supplier has been verified.

Local Storage and Sharing

Saved records are kept by Chrome in the extension's local storage on your device.

Local only

Supplier Analyzer does not transmit captured page data, evidence, or follow-up questions to GEWUJI, a remote server, an analytics service, an advertising network, or another third party.

Your controls

The side panel can save a record, restore saved records, and clear all locally saved captures. The extension keeps up to 20 saved captures; clearing them removes them from the extension's local storage.

Chrome controls the underlying browser storage. If you use browser synchronization, managed profiles, backups, or other browser features, their handling is governed by your Chrome settings and Google's applicable policies.

Retention, Security, and Choices

Saved records remain in local storage until you clear them, the extension clears them as part of a future supported change, or the browser profile removes them. The extension has no server-side copy to delete.

Use the side panel's clear control whenever you no longer need a record. You can also remove the extension or manage its site and storage permissions through Chrome.

No browser storage or software can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep sensitive or confidential information out of the pages you analyze.

Questions

For questions about this extension policy or a privacy request, email llzclm@gmail.com. Please do not include passwords, payment details, or unnecessary confidential materials.

We may update this policy when the extension, website, or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
