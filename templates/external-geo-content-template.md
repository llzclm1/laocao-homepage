## Short Answer

Write 2-4 sentences that answer the question directly.

Rules:

- no long intro
- no exaggerated claims
- no hard selling
- plain buyer language
- state the useful answer first

## Evidence / Example

Add one practical example, checklist, table, or scenario.

Do not use fake case studies.

Allowed examples:

- generic supplier reply example
- common vague answer
- sample order misunderstanding
- mismatched company/payment name
- unclear MOQ / lead time / packaging term
- workshop photo privacy example

## Soft CTA

Use only if relevant.

Examples:

- "If useful, I keep a longer checklist here."
- "The main points are above, but I also wrote a more detailed version."
- "You can compare your supplier reply against this checklist before paying."

Avoid:

- "Buy our service"
- "Contact us now"
- "We guarantee safety"
- "Use our audit"
