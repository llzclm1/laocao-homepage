Hypothetical Example / Supplier Reply Review

Supplier says “best price”: what should a buyer confirm?

This hypothetical example shows how a buyer can turn a simple price statement into clear quotation questions.

It organizes visible supplier communication. It does not judge supplier reliability, price fairness, or a future order outcome.

Review focus
  • What the supplier said
  • What it confirms
  • What is missing
  • What to ask next

Supplier says

The supplier has responded with a price statement, but the basis for comparing that price is not yet visible.

Hypothetical supplier reply

“This is our best price.”

What it confirms

The supplier provided a price statement.

Visible information

The supplier is presenting the current figure as its best offered price for the conversation so far.

What is missing

The buyer still needs the commercial basis behind the price.

Quantity basis

The order volume, MOQ, and any quantity tier used for the price are not stated.

Included items

The product specification, accessories, setup work, or other included items are not listed.

Packaging

The packaging type, labeling, carton requirements, and related costs are not clear.

Payment terms

The payment schedule, currency, and point at which work starts are not stated.

Next question

A useful next question asks the supplier to define the scope before the buyer compares the number.

Ask the supplier

Could you confirm what is included in this price and the quantity basis?

Follow-up details

After the main question, the buyer can request the specific assumptions needed for comparison.

  • QuantityWhich quantity or tier is this price based on?
  • Product scopeWhich material, specification, accessories, and customization are included?
  • PackagingIs the required packaging and labeling included?
  • Commercial termsWhich currency, payment schedule, and shipping basis apply?

What this does not mean

A best-price statement does not show whether two suppliers are quoting the same scope. It also does not establish product quality, supplier reliability, or the final total cost.

Compare the basis first

The immediate task is to make the quantity and included scope visible before comparing prices.

What to remember

“Best price” confirms that a price was offered, but not what the price covers. Ask for the quantity basis, included items, packaging, and payment terms before comparing it with another quotation.
