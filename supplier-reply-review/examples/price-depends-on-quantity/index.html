Hypothetical Example / Supplier Reply Review

Supplier Says “Price Depends on Quantity”: What Should a Buyer Ask Next?

This is a hypothetical educational example created to demonstrate how supplier replies can be reviewed. It is not based on a real buyer or supplier conversation.

The purpose is to separate information provided, missing information, and useful follow-up questions. It does not evaluate supplier reliability, factory capability, price fairness, or payment safety.

Review focus
  • Information provided
  • Information missing
  • Follow-up questions

Introduction

This anonymous, simulated scenario shows how a short supplier reply can be organized before a buyer compares quotations or moves to the next sourcing step.

The review follows the Supplier Reply Review Methodology: identify what the supplier has stated, note what remains unclear, and prepare specific questions.

Scenario

The buyer asks a supplier about product pricing but has not yet provided an expected order quantity.

Hypothetical supplier reply

“Price depends on quantity. Please tell us your order volume.”

Information Provided

The reply provides a small amount of useful commercial context.

  • Expected quantity requestedThe supplier asks the buyer to provide an estimated order volume.
  • Pricing may varyThe supplier indicates that unit pricing may change by quantity.
  • No fixed unit priceA specific unit price has not yet been provided.

Information Missing

The buyer still needs enough detail to understand and compare the quotation.

Order quantity

  • MOQ
  • Price range or quantity tiers

Samples and setup

  • Sample price and terms
  • Tooling or setup costs

Production scope

  • Production lead time
  • Packaging assumptions

Quotation terms

  • Shipping terms or Incoterm
  • Quotation validity

What This Does Not Mean

Missing information does not automatically mean a supplier is problematic. It means the buyer may need clarification before comparing the quotation or moving forward.

  • Supplier reliabilityThe reply does not prove whether the supplier is reliable or unreliable.
  • Factory capabilityThe reply does not confirm production equipment, capacity, or technical capability.
  • Price fairnessThere is not enough information to decide whether a future price is fair.
  • Payment safetyThe reply provides no basis for evaluating payment arrangements or safety.

Follow-up Questions

Specific questions make it easier to compare the same scope across suppliers.

  1. MOQWhat is your MOQ for this product?
  2. Quantity tiersCould you provide unit prices for 100, 500, and 1,000 units?
  3. Sample termsWhat is the sample price, and is any part refundable after a bulk order?
  4. Setup costsAre there any tooling, mold, setup, or customization costs?
  5. Included scopeWhich specifications, materials, packaging, and logo method are included in each price?
  6. Lead timeWhat is the estimated production lead time for each quantity?
  7. Commercial termsWhich currency and Incoterm does the quotation use?

Buyer Reply Template

A concise reply can give the supplier enough context to prepare a more comparable quotation.

Our estimated first order is [quantity] units.

Please quote your MOQ and unit prices for [quantity tiers], including the specification, material, packaging, sample terms, tooling or setup costs, production lead time, currency, and Incoterm.

Key Takeaway

“Price depends on quantity” is limited but useful information: the supplier needs an expected volume before quoting. The buyer still needs quantity tiers, MOQ, included specifications, sample terms, setup costs, lead time, currency, and Incoterm to compare quotations clearly.
