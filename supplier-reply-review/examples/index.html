Where the examples start

Short supplier replies often provide some useful information while leaving important commercial or production details unclear.

The examples use the Supplier Reply Review Methodology on common sourcing conversations. They organize visible information and prepare the next questions without judging supplier reliability.

Examples

Each scenario is simulated. It does not describe a real buyer, supplier, order, payment, or outcome.

Example 001 / Quotation

Supplier says “price depends on quantity”: what should a buyer ask next?

Supplier says: “Price depends on quantity.”

Information provided: Price is related to order volume.

Information missing: MOQ, price tiers, included items, and quotation scope.

Follow-up question: Please provide pricing for the relevant quantity ranges and confirm the included items.

Example 002 / Sample Payment

Supplier requests deposit before sample: what should a buyer clarify?

Supplier says: “Please arrange a deposit before we prepare the sample.”

Information provided: A payment is requested before sample preparation.

Information missing: Sample scope, fee breakdown, payment recipient, and preparation timeline.

Follow-up question: Please confirm the sample scope, each cost, payment recipient, and timing before payment.

Example 003 / Product Capability

Supplier says “we can make any product”: what should a buyer ask next?

Supplier says: “We can make any product.”

Information provided: The supplier is open to discussing customization.

Information missing: Drawings, material, tooling, sample requirements, and production changes.

Follow-up question: Which product details need written confirmation before the sample or production step?

Example 004 / Quotation Scope

Supplier says “best price”: what should a buyer confirm?

Supplier says: “This is our best price.”

Information provided: The supplier provided a price statement.

Information missing: Quantity basis, included items, packaging, and payment terms.

Follow-up question: Could you confirm what is included in this price and the quantity basis?

Example 005 / MOQ Change

Supplier changes MOQ after quotation: what should a buyer ask?

Supplier says: “The MOQ is different now.”

Information provided: The supplier provided an updated quantity condition.

Information missing: Reason for the change, product scope, packaging assumptions, and production conditions.

Follow-up question: Could you explain what changed and which conditions affect the MOQ?
