Common Reasons Buyers Pause Before Payment

Company or payment names do not match

The quotation, invoice, email signature, and beneficiary details use different names without a written explanation.

The price is unusually low

The supplier has not explained the material, packaging, tooling, shipping, or product assumptions behind the number.

The supplier agrees to everything

Replies such as “no problem” or “we can make any product” do not explain actual materials, processes, limitations, or requirements.

Specifications or timing are vague

Terms such as “same quality,” “fast delivery,” or “7 days” do not identify the exact product version or separate production time from delivery time.

Anonymous Example

Supplier: “Yes, we can make it. Sample fee is $120. Delivery 7 days. Please pay first.”

What is still unclear

Material and exact sample version are not confirmed. Logo, packaging, shipping, and payment beneficiary details are missing. “Delivery 7 days” may mean production time rather than arrival time.


Example next question

Could you confirm the material, sample scope, packaging, shipping method, production time, and the company name that will receive payment?
