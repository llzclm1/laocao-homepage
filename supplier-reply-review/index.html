What is a Supplier Reply Review?

A Supplier Reply Review shows what a Chinese supplier has confirmed, what information is still missing, and what questions should be asked next.

Communication support, not verification

It does not verify a supplier or guarantee a transaction. It helps organize supplier communication before the buyer makes a decision.

What Gewuji reviews

Supplier Reply Review helps buyers spot unclear information in supplier communication and decide what to clarify before moving forward.

The review focuses on communication clarity: what has been confirmed, what is missing, what needs clarification, and what the buyer should ask next.

Who this is for

This small-scope review is for buyers who need a second look before sample fees, deposits, tooling payment, or a larger order.

First-time China buyers

First-time buyers sourcing from China, ecommerce sellers, small importers, and buyers preparing their first supplier payment.

Small brands

Example supplier reply

“Yes, we can make it. Sample fee is $120. Delivery 7 days.”


Review notes

Material grade is not confirmed. Logo or customization is not confirmed. Packaging is not confirmed. “Delivery 7 days” may mean sample production time, not arrival time. Payment account name has not been checked against company name.


Suggested next question

Please confirm whether the sample fee includes logo printing, packaging, and shipping. Also confirm the material grade, sample production time, shipping method, and whether the payment account name matches your registered company.

What to ask next

When the reply is broadly positive but the decision is not yet clear, ask:

1. What exact product, quotation, sample, or tooling version does this answer refer to?

2. Which details are confirmed, and which still depend on drawings, approvals, or payment?

3. What costs, responsibilities, or timing assumptions are outside the current reply?

4. What written confirmation should we receive before the next payment or production step?

Common Supplier Reply Patterns

These common phrases can confirm one point while leaving the next sourcing decision open. Use them to prepare a clearer follow-up question.

Supplier says What it confirms What is still unclear Next question
“Price depends on quantity.” Price is related to order volume. MOQ, price tiers, included items, and quotation scope. Please provide pricing for the relevant quantity ranges and confirm the included items.
“Delivery in 7 days.” The supplier provided a timeline. Production time, shipping time, and the point when the timeline starts. Does the seven days refer to production completion or final delivery?
“We can customize.” The supplier accepts customization requests. Drawings, tooling, sample approval, and possible production changes. What needs to be confirmed before production can begin?

FAQ

What is Supplier Reply Review?

Supplier Reply Review helps overseas buyers spot unclear information in Chinese supplier communication and decide what to clarify before moving forward. It focuses on communication clarity, not supplier verification or guarantees.

Does Supplier Reply Review inspect factories?

No. Supplier Reply Review does not inspect factories, audit facilities, verify suppliers, provide legal due diligence, or guarantee payment safety. It reviews supplier communication and points out information gaps.

How should buyers check a Chinese supplier before paying?

Before paying, buyers should check what the supplier has clearly confirmed, what remains missing, whether payment and company details are consistent, and what questions still need written clarification. This review supports that communication check but does not replace audits, inspections, or legal due diligence.

What questions should buyers ask before placing a sample order?

Buyers should ask what the sample includes, whether it matches the future bulk product, what material and packaging are confirmed, what the sample fee covers, how long production and shipping take, and what payment details should be confirmed in writing.

Can a short supplier reply still be reviewed?

Yes. A short reply can still be reviewed for broad commitments, missing product or payment details, unclear timing, and the next questions needed before moving forward.

What if the supplier answers some questions but ignores others?

Treat the unanswered points as open items. List them clearly and ask for written confirmation instead of assuming that silence means agreement.

Can this review compare two supplier quotations?

A focused review can identify whether visible quotation assumptions are comparable, including product scope, material, packaging, MOQ, timing, payment, and exclusions. It does not score supplier reliability or guarantee an outcome.
