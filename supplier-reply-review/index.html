Example supplier reply

“Yes, we can make it. Sample fee is $120. Delivery 7 days.”


Review notes

Material grade is not confirmed. Logo or customization is not confirmed. Packaging is not confirmed. “Delivery 7 days” may mean sample production time, not arrival time. Payment account name has not been checked against company name.


Suggested next question

Please confirm whether the sample fee includes logo printing, packaging, and shipping. Also confirm the material grade, sample production time, shipping method, and whether the payment account name matches your registered company.

FAQ

Is this a factory audit?

No. This is a communication signal review of supplier replies, quotes, sample terms, and payment details. It is not a factory audit, legal due diligence, or quality inspection.

Can you tell me whether the supplier is safe?

No. The review cannot prove a supplier is reliable or safe. It only highlights unclear, missing, inconsistent, or risky communication points and suggests next questions.

What kind of supplier reply can I send?

You can send a supplier reply, quotation, proforma invoice, sample terms, payment account detail, product specification discussion, packaging discussion, MOQ or lead time discussion, and relevant factory photos or videos.

Can you help write the next message to the supplier?

Yes. The report can include a clear English message the buyer can send back to ask for missing details and written confirmation.

Should I use this before paying a deposit?

It can be useful before paying a sample fee, deposit, tooling money, or larger order if the supplier reply still has unclear terms, missing details, or inconsistent information.
