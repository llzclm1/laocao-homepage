Example Supplier Reply

“Yes, we can make it. Sample fee is $120. Delivery 7 days. MOQ 500 pcs. We can add your logo. Please pay sample fee first.”

Important boundary

This is not a real case. It is a generic example used to show the report format.

1. Supplier summary

  • They say they can make the product.
  • Sample fee is quoted as $120.
  • MOQ is stated as 500 pcs.
  • Logo customization is mentioned.
  • Delivery is stated as 7 days.

2. Missing information

  • Material grade is not confirmed.
  • Product size, tolerance, finish, and exact version are not confirmed.
  • Logo method is not confirmed.
  • Packaging is not confirmed.
  • Shipping cost and shipping method are not confirmed.
  • “Delivery 7 days” may mean production time, not arrival time.
  • Payment account name has not been matched with company name.
  • Sample-to-bulk differences are not confirmed.

3. Risk signals

  • Supplier asks for payment before key sample details are confirmed.
  • “We can make it” is too broad without technical confirmation.
  • Logo customization is mentioned but not specified.
  • MOQ is stated, but bulk price and packaging assumptions are not clear.
  • Delivery term is vague.

4. Next questions

  • Please confirm the exact material grade, product size, finish, and sample version.
  • Does the sample fee include logo printing, packaging, and shipping? If not, please separate each cost.
  • What logo method will be used for the sample and bulk order?
  • Does “delivery 7 days” mean sample production time or arrival time at my address?
  • Please confirm the shipping method and estimated shipping cost.
  • Please confirm whether the payment account name matches your registered company name.
  • Will the bulk product use the same material, logo method, packaging, and finish as the sample?

5. Suggested reply

A clear reply can slow the decision down just enough to confirm the terms that matter before payment.

Hi [Supplier Name],


Thank you for the quote.


Before I pay the sample fee, please help confirm a few details:


1. Exact material grade, size, finish, and sample version

2. Whether the $120 sample fee includes logo printing, packaging, and shipping

3. Logo method for both sample and bulk production

4. Whether “delivery 7 days” means sample production time or arrival time

5. Shipping method and estimated shipping cost

6. Whether the payment account name matches your registered company name

7. Whether the bulk order will use the same material, logo method, packaging, and finish as the sample


Once these details are clear, I can review the sample order more confidently.


Best,
[Buyer Name]

6. Decision note

Do not treat this reply as enough information for payment yet.

The buyer can continue the conversation, but should confirm the missing sample, packaging, shipping, payment account, and bulk production details before paying.


This does not mean the supplier is unsafe. It means the reply is not specific enough yet.

7. What this report cannot prove

This report cannot prove:

  • whether the supplier is reliable
  • whether the factory is real
  • whether the company is legally verified
  • whether the product quality is safe
  • whether payment is protected
  • whether the order will be delivered correctly

It only helps identify unclear communication, missing terms, visible risk signals, and next questions.
