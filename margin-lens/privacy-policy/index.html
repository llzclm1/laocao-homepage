Margin Lens Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the Margin Lens Chrome extension handles information from a public product page selected by you.

Effective date: September 20, 2026. This policy applies to Margin Lens, not to the separate Gewuji website services.

In brief
  • Reading starts after your toolbar action.
  • Scenario settings stay in Chrome local storage.
  • No page HTML upload, login, order flow, or remote API.

What Margin Lens Does

Margin Lens is a local-first calculator for estimating one-unit cross-border product economics from the public product page you are viewing. It shows page facts, source traces, missing or conflicting values, and calculations such as estimated profit, margin, break-even price, CAC, and CPC.

It does not provide supplier verification, tax advice, logistics quotes, advertising advice, or a guarantee that a page claim or calculation is accurate.

Information Read After Your Action

When you click the Margin Lens toolbar button on a normal HTTP(S) product page, the extension may read publicly visible product information from that current page.

  • Page contextPage URL, title, hostname, platform marker, and capture time.
  • Product fieldsProcurement price, retail price, MOQ, weight, package dimensions, product dimensions, SKU or model, and supplier name when readable.
  • Source evidenceReadable excerpts and DOM or JSON-LD locators used to show where a field came from.

Page values remain source claims, not independently verified facts. Missing procurement prices are not converted to zero. Conflicts and partial values remain marked for review.

Local Storage and Processing

Margin Lens performs the MVP calculation in the browser.

Scenario settings

FX assumptions, selling price, freight, platform fee, advertising, returns, payment fee, import tax, and related settings are saved in Chrome storage.local so the scenario can be restored after a refresh.

Current page session

The extracted page snapshot is used to render the current side panel and calculate the scenario. This MVP does not upload page HTML or send the snapshot to a Margin Lens server.

You can use Reset in the side panel to restore default scenario settings, or remove locally stored extension data through Chrome.

Permissions and Boundaries

  • activeTabAccess the current page only after the user invokes the toolbar action.
  • scriptingInject the reader bundled with the extension into the user-selected page.
  • sidePanelDisplay page facts, evidence, inputs, and calculations in the Chrome side panel.
  • storageSave and restore local scenario settings in Chrome.

Margin Lens does not request <all_urls> or host permissions. It does not read passwords, payment details, cookies, browsing history, or pages outside the current user-selected tab. It does not include remote code, login, checkout, order placement, messaging, or account workflows.

Network, Sharing, and Retention

The MVP does not call exchange-rate, logistics, tax, advertising, or other third-party data APIs. It does not sell, share, or transmit page information to a remote service operated by Margin Lens.

Information generated for the current side panel stays in the browser session unless you independently copy or record it. Scenario settings remain in Chrome local storage until reset, extension data is cleared, or the extension is removed.

Changes and Contact

We may update this policy if Margin Lens changes its data handling. The effective date at the top will identify the current version.
