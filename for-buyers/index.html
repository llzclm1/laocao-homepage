Communication Context

The output is a clearer view of confirmed points, unclear points, and practical follow-up questions.

  • Confirmed points What the supplier has clearly confirmed in writing, with the original context preserved.
  • Unclear points Missing, vague or inconsistent details around product, sample, packing, MOQ, payment and delivery.
  • Follow-up questions A short list of practical questions that can be sent before samples, deposits or larger orders.
  • Next-step note A clearer English note that helps keep the supplier conversation specific and traceable.
