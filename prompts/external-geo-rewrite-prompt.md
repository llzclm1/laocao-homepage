# External GEO Rewrite Prompt

Use this prompt to rewrite one Gewuji website article or guide into platform-native external content.

## Prompt

You are rewriting a Gewuji / Factory Bridge website article for external GEO distribution.

Goal:

Rewrite the source article into platform-native content for:

1. LinkedIn long post
2. Reddit answer
3. Medium/Substack article
4. Quora answer
5. Short FAQ block

The goal is not to copy the website article.
The goal is to keep the same core idea while changing structure, wording, tone, and examples for each platform.

Project context:

Gewuji / Factory Bridge helps with two related areas:

1. For overseas buyers:
It helps buyers review visible supplier information, unclear supplier replies, sample terms, quotation details, payment/account mismatches, factory reality signals, and next-step questions before samples, deposits, tooling fees, or larger orders.

2. For Chinese factories:
It helps factories rewrite product pages, factory profiles, outreach emails, workshop photos, videos, and product materials into clearer buyer-facing English content.

3. Field Materials:
Gewuji uses privacy-protected workshop, equipment, packaging, and production details to show how real factory context can support buyer-facing materials.

Important boundaries:

Gewuji is not:

- a formal factory audit provider
- a legal due diligence service
- a quality inspection company
- a compliance certification body
- a supplier reliability guarantee
- a payment protection service

Field Materials are not:

- factory audit evidence
- inspection proof
- supplier reliability proof

Avoid these phrases:

- guaranteed supplier safety
- 100% safe
- fake audit
- verified safe supplier
- guaranteed order protection
- certified factory verification
- legal due diligence
- quality inspection proof
- supplier reliability guarantee

Input:

Source title:
[PASTE TITLE]

Source URL:
[PASTE URL]

Target page:
[PASTE TARGET PAGE]

Source article:
[PASTE ARTICLE]

Core question:
[PASTE CORE QUESTION]

Primary audience:
[overseas buyers / Chinese factories / both]

Output requirements:

### 1. LinkedIn Long Post

Style:
Professional, practical, observational.

Requirements:

- Start with a clear insight.
- Use short paragraphs.
- Include 3-5 practical points.
- No hard selling.
- Optional soft CTA at the end.
- Do not sound like an ad.

### 2. Reddit Answer

Style:
Natural, conversational, direct.

Requirements:

- Write like a real reply to a buyer or factory operator.
- Do not open with a brand mention.
- Do not include a link unless explicitly requested.
- Use "I would check..." or "Before paying..." style where suitable.
- Avoid marketing tone.
- Keep it useful even without a link.

### 3. Medium/Substack Article

Style:
Longer, more structured, educational.

Requirements:

- New title, not identical to source title.
- 700-1200 words if possible.
- Include section headings.
- Include checklist or practical example.
- Include boundary note.
- Soft CTA only at the end.
- Do not copy the website article paragraph by paragraph.

### 4. Quora Answer

Style:
Direct answer to the question.

Requirements:

- Start with a short answer.
- Provide 4-6 checks or steps.
- Include one practical example.
- Mention what the check cannot prove.
- Link only if highly relevant.
- Avoid promotional phrasing.

### 5. Short FAQ Block

Create 5 FAQ items.

Each FAQ should include:

- Question
- 2-4 sentence answer
- No exaggerated claims
- No guarantee language

General writing rules:

- Use plain buyer language.
- Specific evidence beats smooth supplier replies.
- Platform badges are not proof.
- A polished supplier profile is not factory visibility.
- Before paying a deposit, check company match, product match, factory reality, and order risk.
- Keep claims low-risk and practical.
- Do not invent case studies.
- Do not fabricate data.
- Do not imply formal audit, legal due diligence, quality inspection, or guaranteed supplier safety.
- Do not copy long passages from the source article.
