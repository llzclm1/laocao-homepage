## Script

A fast supplier reply is not proof.

Before you pay a Chinese supplier, slow down and check the reply carefully.

First, does the company name match the payment account?

Second, is the sample version clearly written, including model, material, size, or color?

Third, are packaging and logo details included, or still unclear?

Fourth, when they say "7 days delivery", does that mean production time, shipping time, or arrival time?

Fifth, will bulk production match the approved sample, or can details change later?

These checks do not prove whether a supplier is reliable.

They only help you find unclear points before payment.
