### Thumbnail Text

Before You Pay

### Voiceover

Before paying a Chinese supplier, don't only ask if they can make it.

Check what is actually confirmed in writing.

Does the company name match the quotation, invoice, email signature, and payment account?

If the payment account is different, ask for a written explanation before sending money.

Then confirm the sample details, packaging, logo method, and what "delivery time" means.

Also ask what may change between the sample and bulk production.

This does not confirm supplier reliability. It helps you find unclear points before you pay.

Video Is Not Proof

### Voiceover

A factory video can be useful, but it is not proof of everything.

It may show machines, workers, packaging, cartons, or a production line.

Those are useful signals.

But the video does not prove ownership, quality stability, delivery reliability, or whether the legal company matches the seller.

Use the video to ask better questions.

Ask what product is being made, what is outsourced, and what the video does not show.

It is not a replacement for formal supplier checks.

### Thumbnail Text

Yes Is Not Enough

### Voiceover

When a supplier says, "Yes, we can make it," slow down for a moment.

"Yes" is not the same as order confirmation.

Ask what material they will use.

Ask the exact size, finish, logo method, and packaging.

Ask whether the sample lead time is for a stock sample, similar sample, or custom sample.

Then ask what may change before bulk production.

A smooth yes can be helpful, but it does not replace clear written details.

Sample Questions

### Voiceover

Before ordering a sample from China, make the sample purpose clear.

Is it checking the material, size, finish, logo, packaging, or production ability?

Ask whether the sample is stock, similar, or custom made.

Ask what will be different in bulk production.

Ask who pays shipping, what the lead time means, and whether the same packaging will be used later.

A sample is useful only when you know what it is supposed to prove.

### Thumbnail Text

Video Call Checklist

### Voiceover

On a factory video call, do not only ask, "Can you show me the factory?"

Ask for specific areas.

Ask to see the sample room, similar products, packaging area, labels, cartons, and the main production flow.

Ask which steps are done in-house and which steps may be outsourced.

Also ask the seller to show the product close to the camera and explain what is different from your order.

The goal is not to prove everything on a call.

The goal is to find better next questions.
