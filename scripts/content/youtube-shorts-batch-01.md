## Short 1

### Title

Don't Pay a Chinese Supplier Before Checking This

### Source

`https://gewuji.dev/buyer-guides/verify-chinese-supplier-before-deposit/`

### Hook

Before paying a Chinese supplier, don't only ask if they can make it.

### Script

Before sending a deposit, check what is actually confirmed in writing.

Does the company name match the quotation, invoice, email signature, and payment account?

Does the payment account belong to the same company, or did the supplier explain the difference clearly?

For the product, confirm the sample details, packaging, logo method, and what "delivery time" really means.

Also ask what may change between the sample and bulk production.

This does not prove the supplier is safe. It just helps you find unclear points before you pay.

## Short 2

### Title

A Factory Video Does Not Prove Everything

### Source

`https://gewuji.dev/field-materials/`

### Hook

A factory video can be useful, but it is not proof of everything.

### Script

A supplier video may show machines, workers, packaging, cartons, or a production line.

That can give you useful signals.

But the video does not prove who owns the factory, whether quality will be stable, whether delivery will be reliable, or whether the legal company matches the seller.

Use the video to ask better questions.

Ask what product is being made, whether similar products are current orders, what is outsourced, and what the video does not show.

It is not a replacement for a factory audit or quality inspection.

## Short 3

### Title

When a Supplier Says "Yes, We Can Make It"

### Source

`https://gewuji.dev/buyer-guides/china-supplier-red-flags-before-first-order/`

### Hook

"Yes, we can make it" is not the same as order confirmation.

### Script

When a supplier says, "Yes, we can make it," slow down for a moment.

Ask what material they will use.

Ask the exact size, finish, logo method, and packaging.

Ask whether the sample lead time is for a stock sample, similar sample, or custom sample.

Then ask what may change before bulk production.

A smooth yes can be helpful, but it does not replace clear written details.

## Short 4

### Title

Before Ordering Samples from China, Ask This

### Source

`https://gewuji.dev/buyer-guides/questions-before-ordering-samples-from-china/`

### Hook

Before paying for a sample, ask what the sample is supposed to prove.

### Script

Before ordering samples from China, don't only ask the price.

Ask whether the sample is the exact product version you want.

Ask whether packaging, logo, color, material, and customization are included.

Ask the sample lead time and how shipping is handled.

Most important, ask what may still change in bulk production.

The sample should reduce uncertainty, not create a new set of assumptions.

## Short 5

### Title

How to Use a Chinese Factory Video Call

### Source

`https://gewuji.dev/buyer-guides/chinese-factory-video-call-checklist/`

### Hook

On a factory video call, don't just say, "show me the factory."

### Script

Ask for a live video call, not only saved clips.

Ask to see the sample room, similar products, packaging area, labels, cartons, and the basic production flow.

If some steps are outsourced, ask which steps and where they happen.

But keep the boundary clear.

A video call cannot prove everything about ownership, quality, delivery, or payment safety.

It helps you ask better next questions before samples, deposits, or first orders.
