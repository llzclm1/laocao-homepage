# AI-Generated YouTube Production Route for Gewuji Supplier Review

这份路线定义 Gewuji Supplier Review 第一阶段“不口播、不露脸、不拍摄”的全 AI 视频生产方式。目标是低成本、可重复、边界安全，而不是做 AI 视频垃圾号。

## 1. Decision

Gewuji Supplier Review will not rely on face camera videos, live talking-head recordings, factory tour footage, or personal vlogs in the first stage.

The first stage uses:

- AI-generated voiceover
- programmatic video templates
- text overlays
- generic supplier reply examples
- checklist cards
- illustrative industrial visuals
- sample report walkthroughs

The goal is not to look like a viral AI faceless channel.

The goal is to create clear, repeatable, boundary-safe trust assets for overseas buyers.

## 2. Core Production Principle

AI can generate:

- draft scripts
- scene plans
- captions
- voiceover audio
- illustrative visuals
- video layout
- thumbnails
- descriptions

Human must review:

- supplier risk interpretation
- boundary language
- no guarantee claims
- no audit/legal/inspection claims
- CTA wording
- privacy safety
- whether visuals may mislead buyers

Core rule:

```text
AI produces the video assets.
Human reviews the business judgment and boundary language.
```

## 3. Recommended Tool Stack

### 3.1 Remotion Skills as Main Stack

Use Remotion for:

- fixed Shorts templates
- checklist animations
- supplier reply breakdown videos
- sample report walkthroughs
- consistent captions
- consistent CTA cards
- batch generation from JSON

Example JSON fields:

- title
- hook
- supplier_reply
- confirmed_points
- missing_points
- risk_signals
- next_questions
- boundary_note
- cta
- source_page
- description_links

### 3.2 HyperFrames as Secondary Stack

Use HyperFrames for:

- fast social video drafts
- animated intros
- simple product/service explainers
- LinkedIn short videos
- visual experiments

Do not use it to generate sensational or vague AI content.

### 3.3 Generative Media Skills with Limits

Allowed use:

- abstract industrial backgrounds
- non-realistic illustrations
- icons
- neutral diagrams
- thumbnail background textures

Not allowed:

- fake real factory footage
- fake workers
- fake customer scenes
- fake inspection scenes
- fake legal/payment risk scenes
- visuals that imply actual supplier proof

If AI visuals are used, descriptions may include:

```text
Visuals are illustrative. Supplier checks should be based on real documents, replies, photos, and videos from the supplier.
```

### 3.4 Tools Not Needed in Stage One

video-use:

Not needed in stage one because there is no talking-head footage or interview footage.

videocut-skills:

Not needed in stage one because Gewuji Supplier Review is an English B2B channel, not a Chinese short-video口播 workflow.

## 4. Best Video Formats

### Format 1: Supplier Reply Breakdown

Visual:

generic supplier message card

Example:

```text
Yes, we can make it. Sample fee is $120. Delivery 7 days.
```

Sections:

- what is confirmed
- what is missing
- what to ask next
- boundary note
- CTA

Best for:

- `/supplier-reply-review/`
- `/supplier-reply-review/sample-report/`

### Format 2: Before You Pay Checklist

Visual:

checklist cards

Sections:

- company name
- payment account
- sample version
- packaging
- delivery meaning
- sample-to-bulk difference

Best for:

- buyer guides
- Shorts

### Format 3: Factory Video Signal Explainer

Visual:

abstract or blurred industrial background + text cards

Sections:

- what the video may show
- what it cannot prove
- what to ask next

Best for:

- `/field-materials/`
- factory video call checklist

### Format 4: Sample Report Walkthrough

Visual:

sample report cards

Sections:

- supplier summary
- missing information
- risk signals
- next questions
- suggested reply
- decision note

Best for:

- `/supplier-reply-review/sample-report/`

## 5. AI Voiceover Rules

Voice style:

- clear international English
- calm
- practical
- consultant-like
- medium speed
- low emotional intensity
- no fear-based tone
- no salesy tone

Avoid:

- trailer voice
- influencer hype
- dramatic warning tone
- urgent scam tone
- fake personal story tone

## 6. Visual Safety Rules

Do not create visuals that look like actual evidence from a real supplier unless they are truly anonymized and allowed.

Avoid showing:

- factory names
- customer names
- logos
- drawings
- order numbers
- faces
- labels
- private production details
- fake factory operations
- fake inspection scenes

Prefer:

- neutral cards
- blurred backgrounds
- diagrams
- checklists
- generic chat boxes
- sample report excerpts
- illustrative icons

## 7. Production Workflow

1. Select source page.
2. Define buyer situation.
3. Extract one unique angle.
4. Write Short or explainer script.
5. Convert script into video JSON.
6. Generate or select AI voiceover.
7. Render with Remotion or HyperFrames.
8. Generate captions.
9. Generate thumbnail text.
10. Human boundary review.
11. Export MP4.
12. Publish manually.
13. Record metrics.

## 9. Stage One Success Criteria

成功不看爆款播放，而看：

- video can be produced repeatedly
- visual style feels professional
- voiceover is acceptable
- no misleading visuals
- no boundary violations
- CTA is clear but not salesy
- video can link back to `/supplier-reply-review/sample-report/`
- production time is reasonable

## 10. What Not To Do

- do not install every video skill at once
- do not generate fake factory footage
- do not auto-publish videos
- do not make 10-minute AdSense-first videos
- do not imitate scam/exposed channels
- do not turn Gewuji into a content farm
- do not let AI decide whether a supplier is safe
- do not use guarantee language
