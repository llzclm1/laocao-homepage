# Boundary Checklist

Use this checklist before any page, social draft, or monitoring summary moves forward.

## Must Say

- Gewuji helps buyers understand supplier information.
- Gewuji helps factories make materials easier for overseas buyers to understand.
- Supplier Reply Review identifies confirmed information, unclear information, missing details, and next questions.
- Field Materials provide visible context, not proof.

## Must Not Say

- We verify suppliers.
- We audit factories.
- We inspect product quality.
- We protect payments.
- We guarantee supplier reliability.
- A clear sample, quotation, or reply means a supplier is safe.

## Decision

| Result | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `approved` | Boundary is clear and useful |
| `needs_revision` | Language drifts into verification, audit, inspection, guarantee, or payment safety |

## Fast Review Questions

1. Does this answer a real buyer or factory question?
2. Does it identify missing information?
3. Does it give next questions?
4. Does it avoid supplier verification language?
5. Does it avoid audit, inspection, quality, and payment safety claims?
