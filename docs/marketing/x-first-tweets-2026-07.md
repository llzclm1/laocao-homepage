# Supplier Reply Review 第一批 X 推文：2026-07

这些推文用于测试 China supply chain observation 账号方向。风格保持短、专业、不营销。

1. A fast supplier reply is not always a clear supplier reply. Before payment, separate what is confirmed from what is still unclear.

2. Before paying a Chinese supplier deposit, check: payment account name, invoice name, exact product version, packaging, and lead time.

3. “Yes, we can do it” is not a technical answer. Ask for material grade, tolerance, finish, logo method, and MOQ impact.

4. A factory video is useful context. It is not proof of everything. Ask to see the area related to your actual product.

5. A sample is only useful when the sample scope is clear. What does it prove? What can still change in bulk?

6. Low quote is not the issue. Low quote plus unclear scope is the issue.

7. If packaging is “discuss later,” the quote may not be comparable yet.

8. “7 days delivery” can mean production time, shipping time, or arrival time. Ask which one.

9. Platform badges are signals. They do not replace reading the actual supplier reply carefully.

10. Before moving forward with a supplier, ask: what did they confirm, what is unclear, and what should I ask next?

11. Payment account mismatch does not prove anything by itself. It does need written clarification before payment.

12. Sample approval should create a written baseline for bulk production, not just a good feeling.

13. Better supplier answers often start with better buyer questions: short, specific, and easy to answer.

14. Factory vs trading company is not always the main question. Ask what the supplier actually controls.

15. The first order should be small, specific, and boring. Ambiguity scales badly.
