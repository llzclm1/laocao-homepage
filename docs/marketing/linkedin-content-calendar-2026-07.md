# LinkedIn 内容计划：Supplier Reply Review，2026-07

30 天 LinkedIn 内容计划。语气：专业观察，不销售，不保证结果。

| Day | Topic | Hook | Main points | CTA | Target audience |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Supplier reply clarity | A fast supplier reply is not always a clear supplier reply. | Fast replies can still miss material, packaging, payment, sample scope. Ask for written confirmation. | What do you usually check before sample payment? | ecommerce founders, Amazon sellers |

| 9 | Sample fee | Before paying a sample fee, ask what is included. | Logo, packaging, shipping, sample version, delivery meaning. | Confirm the sample scope. | Amazon sellers |
| 10 | Lead time | “7 days delivery” can mean several different things. | Production time vs shipping time vs arrival time. | Ask what the clock starts from. | small importers |
