# Buyer Guides 内容路线图

这份文档用于定义 `/buyer-guides/` 的首批内容范围、统一结构和后续扩展顺序，避免临时想到什么就写什么。

## 栏目定位

`/buyer-guides/`：

Practical China supplier communication guides for overseas buyers before samples, deposits, tooling fees, and bulk orders.

栏目职责：

- 吃英文搜索长尾
- 给 Quora / Reddit / LinkedIn / X 提供拆分母稿
- 明确服务边界，避免用户把页面当成审厂或保证服务

## 统一文章结构

每篇文章使用同一结构：

1. Short answer
2. Why this matters
3. Step-by-step checklist
4. Common red flags
5. Example supplier questions
6. What this cannot prove
7. Related buyer guides
8. Soft CTA

## 统一 Soft CTA

```text
Need a second look at a supplier reply?

Gewuji can help review visible supplier information, unclear points, and next-step questions before samples, deposits, or larger orders.

This is not a formal factory audit, legal due diligence, quality inspection, or supplier reliability guarantee.
```

## 内容优先级

第一优先：

- verify before deposit
- red flags before first order
- compare quotations beyond price
- questions before ordering samples

原因：

- 最容易对应真实搜索词
- 最容易拆成 Quora / Reddit / LinkedIn / X
- 最接近当前已有的对外回复语料

第二优先：

- factory is real
- video call checklist
- factory or trading company
- supplier badges

第三优先：

- tooling money
- sample order
- certificates
- supplier documents

## 写作边界

- 文章只写 visible supplier information、unclear points、next-step questions
- 不写“we verify the supplier is safe”
- 不写“we guarantee reliability”
- 不把页面包装成 formal audit、legal due diligence、quality inspection
