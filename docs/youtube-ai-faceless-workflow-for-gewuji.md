# AI Faceless Video Workflow Adapted for Gewuji Supplier Review

This SOP adapts the AI faceless YouTube production workflow for Gewuji Supplier Review without turning the channel into an entertainment, hype, or content-farm channel.

## 1. Core Judgment

The AI faceless channel workflow is useful as a production framework, but not as a positioning model for Gewuji.

Gewuji should not copy entertainment, history, survival, mystery, or low-quality animation channels.

Gewuji can borrow the workflow:

- topic research
- title framework extraction
- unique angle development
- script drafting
- scene planning
- voiceover
- editing
- thumbnail templates
- analytics review

But the channel must remain:

- practical
- calm
- buyer-focused
- non-sensational
- boundary-safe
- service-aligned

Gewuji's YouTube direction is low-cost production, high professional boundary, long-tail search, service conversion, and trust asset.

## 2. What Gewuji Can Learn From AI Faceless Channels

### 2.1 Title Framework Extraction

Do not copy another channel's content. Extract title frameworks and adapt them to real buyer questions.

Example generic title frameworks:

- Before you do [action], check this first
- Why [common assumption] is not enough
- What nobody tells you about [specific risk]
- The hidden risk in [buyer situation]
- When [supplier behavior] happens, ask this

Adapted Gewuji examples:

- Before Paying a Chinese Supplier, Check This First
- Why "Yes, We Can Make It" Is Not Enough
- What Nobody Tells You About Factory Videos from China
- The Hidden Risk in a Very Low China Supplier Quote
- When a Supplier Reply Is Fast but Vague, Ask This

### 2.2 Unique Angle Before Script

Do not ask AI to "write a YouTube script" directly.

Every video needs a specific Gewuji judgment angle before the script is written.

Examples:

- A supplier reply is not risky because it is short. It is risky when key commercial details are undefined.
- A factory video is useful only when you know what question the video is supposed to answer.
- A low quote is not automatically bad. The real issue is whether the supplier can explain the price difference clearly.
- Sample approval does not confirm bulk production unless material, logo, packaging, finish, and tolerance are written.

### 2.3 Scene Generation

Gewuji videos do not need cinematic animation.

Preferred scenes:

- generic supplier reply screenshot style
- highlighted missing information
- checklist cards
- blurred factory background
- sample report snippets
- simple icons
- before / after question table

Avoid:

- fake factory footage
- sensational scam visuals
- cartoonish low-trust animation
- exposed real customer/factory details

### 2.4 AI Voiceover

AI voiceover can be tested, but the tone must be:

- calm
- professional
- consultant-like
- clear
- not dramatic
- not salesy

Avoid:

- overhyped voice
- fear-based narration
- clickbait tone
- fake urgency

### 2.5 Thumbnail Templates

Use 3-4 fixed thumbnail templates so production stays simple and recognizable.

Template A:

```text
DON'T PAY YET
5 supplier checks
```

Template B:

```text
FACTORY VIDEO?
What it cannot prove
```

Template C:

```text
YES, WE CAN
Still unclear
```

Template D:

```text
LOW QUOTE?
Ask why
```

Visual elements:

- white / grey background
- supplier chat bubble
- checklist mark
- small warning icon
- blurred factory image
- red underline or highlight

Avoid:

- SCAM
- EXPOSED
- CHINA FACTORY SECRET
- YOU WILL LOSE MONEY
- fake police / danger graphics

### 2.6 Repurposing System

Each video should come from an existing asset:

- buyer guide
- supplier reply review sample report
- field materials
- external GEO post
- FAQ block

Each video can also be repurposed back into:

- LinkedIn post
- Reddit answer
- Quora answer
- website FAQ
- short checklist
- sample report section

### 2.7 Data Review

Do not judge by views only.

Track:

- views
- retention
- average view duration
- website clicks
- YouTube referral traffic
- comments with supplier-specific questions
- visits to `/supplier-reply-review/`
- visits to `/supplier-reply-review/sample-report/`
- topics that bring qualified interest

## 3. What Gewuji Should Not Copy

Do not copy:

1. "$60k in 30 days" expectations
2. Low-quality animation style
3. fully automated AI content
4. copying another channel's exact niche
5. sensational thumbnails
6. fake stories
7. vague AI scripts
8. entertainment pacing that damages trust
9. long 10-minute videos only for ads
10. AdSense-first strategy

Reason:

Gewuji is not an entertainment channel. It is a B2B trust and service channel.

## 4. Gewuji YouTube Production SOP

### Step 1: Select Source Asset

Choose one:

- existing buyer guide
- sample report section
- field materials section
- real but anonymized recurring buyer question
- external GEO draft

### Step 2: Define Buyer Situation

Write one concrete buyer situation.

Example:

```text
The buyer is about to pay a sample fee after receiving a vague supplier reply.
```

### Step 3: Extract One Unique Judgment

Write the one judgment the video should make.

Example:

```text
The problem is not the sample fee itself. The problem is paying before the sample version, packaging, shipping, logo method, and account name are confirmed.
```

### Step 4: Choose Format

Choose:

- Short, 30-45 seconds
- Explainer, 3-5 minutes

Do not start with 10-minute videos.

### Step 5: Write Hook

Examples:

- A fast supplier reply is not proof.
- A factory video does not prove everything.
- "Yes, we can make it" is not a confirmed order detail.
- A low quote is not the problem. A vague explanation is.

### Step 6: Write Script

Required structure:

- Hook
- short answer
- 3-5 checks
- generic example
- boundary note
- soft CTA

### Step 7: Scene Plan

Scene types:

- title card
- supplier message card
- highlighted missing details
- checklist card
- what it cannot prove card
- next questions card
- CTA card

### Step 8: Voiceover

Use either:

- own voice
- AI voiceover

Voice rules:

- calm
- clear
- English
- not dramatic
- no fear-based delivery

### Step 9: Edit

Use simple editing:

- captions
- slow zoom
- highlight boxes
- checklist transitions
- no excessive effects

### Step 10: Thumbnail

Use one of the fixed templates.

### Step 11: Description

Include:

- 2-3 sentence summary
- relevant source link
- supplier reply review link
- sample report link if relevant
- boundary note

### Step 12: Publish and Track

Record:

- title
- URL
- date
- source page
- format
- views
- retention
- website clicks
- comments
- next action

## 7. Boundary Checklist Before Publishing

Before publishing any video, confirm:

- no supplier guarantee language
- no factory audit claim
- no legal due diligence claim
- no quality inspection claim
- no "verified safe supplier"
- no exposed factory/customer identity
- no fake case
- no fake factory footage
- no sensational scam framing
- CTA points to sample report or relevant buyer guide
- description includes boundary-safe wording

## 8. Final Recommendation

The AI faceless workflow should be used to reduce production friction, not to turn Gewuji into a content farm.

The goal is not to produce many videos quickly.

The goal is to produce clear, repeatable, trust-building videos that support:

- supplier reply review
- buyer guides
- field materials
- external GEO distribution
- long-tail search visibility
