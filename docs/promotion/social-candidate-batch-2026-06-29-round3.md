## 推荐明天内容

### X 原创短帖 1

```text
A fast supplier reply feels good, but it is not proof.

Before trusting the quote, check who you are paying, what product they actually control, and whether the sample, packing, and company details match.
```

### X 原创短帖 2

```text
A video call can show a workshop.

It still does not prove who owns the order, who handles defects, or whether the bulk goods will match the approved sample.

Use video as one check, not the whole check.
```

```text

Before payment, the buyer still has leverage. After payment, every unclear detail becomes harder to fix.
```
