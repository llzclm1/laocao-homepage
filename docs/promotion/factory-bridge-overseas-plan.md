# China Factory Bridge 海外推广计划

目标：先给 `for-buyers` 带来真实海外买家访问，再用少量内容验证 `for-factories` 是否能承接工厂端需求。

主入口：

- Buyers: https://gewuji.dev/for-buyers/
- Factories: https://gewuji.dev/for-factories/
- Field materials: https://gewuji.dev/field-materials/

不做：

- 国内社媒
- 账号矩阵
- 付费广告
- 硬广式 Reddit / Quora 链接投放

## 1. LinkedIn

频率：每周 3 条英文短帖。

发布账号：优先个人号，不先做公司号。

内容方向：

1. Supplier red flags
2. Factory reality check examples
3. Before paying deposit questions
4. How to read supplier photos
5. Jiangsu / Zhejiang / Shanghai factory visit notes
6. What a lightweight supplier check can and cannot do

固定结构：

```text
Problem:
One real sourcing confusion.

Observation:
What buyers should check before moving forward.

Practical question:
One question to ask the supplier.

Soft link:
For a lightweight China factory reality check:
https://gewuji.dev/for-buyers/
```

首批 6 条标题：

- 5 red flags before paying a Chinese supplier deposit
- A polished supplier page is not the same as factory visibility
- What I look for in a basic China factory reality check
- Before ordering samples, ask these 7 supplier questions
- What factory photos can tell you, and what they cannot
- Why small buyers need practical supplier notes, not a full audit

## 3. Reddit / Quora

阶段：第 2 周以后再做。

原则：

- 只回答真实问题
- 先给完整答案，再放链接
- 同一个社区一周最多 1 次链接
- 不复制粘贴同一段

可关注问题类型：

- How do I verify a Chinese supplier?
- Is this Alibaba supplier real?
- Should I pay a deposit before visiting a factory?
- How can I check a factory in China remotely?
- What questions should I ask before ordering samples?

回答模板：

```text
Short answer:
Do not rely on the listing alone.

What I would check:
1. Supplier address and business consistency
2. Workshop / warehouse / product sample evidence
3. MOQ, lead time, sample process and customization answers
4. Photos or videos that match the product category
5. Red flags in communication

Boundary:
This is not a formal audit or legal due diligence.

Optional:
I keep a lightweight factory reality check page here:
https://gewuji.dev/for-buyers/
```
