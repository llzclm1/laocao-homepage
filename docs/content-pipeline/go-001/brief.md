# GO-001 Content Brief

## SEO Title

Questions Before Ordering Samples From China: Buyer Checklist

## Meta Description

Ask these questions before ordering samples from China to clarify product specs, sample type, fees, lead time, shipping, packaging, and what the sample does not prove.

## H1

Questions Before Ordering Samples From China

## TL;DR

Before ordering a sample from a Chinese supplier, ask what the sample will prove, what it will not prove, and which details still need confirmation before bulk production. Focus on product specs, sample type, customization, packaging, sample fee, lead time, shipping cost, and next-step quotation basis.

This is a communication clarity step. It is not supplier verification, a factory audit, quality inspection, payment protection, or a supplier reliability guarantee.

## Target Reader

Overseas buyers who are considering a sample order from a Chinese supplier and want to avoid unclear sample scope, unexpected costs, or false confidence before bulk production.

## Page Structure

### H2: Direct Answer

Give the short answer first: ask what the sample will prove, what it will not prove, and which bulk-order details remain unconfirmed.

### H2: Why Sample Questions Matter

Explain that the risk is not only the sample itself. The risk is unclear expectations before the buyer pays, ships, or assumes the supplier understands the future order.

### H2: Questions About Product Specification

#### H3: Which exact product details should be confirmed?

Cover model, material, dimensions, color, finish, accessory, compliance note if relevant, and tolerance if applicable.

### H2: Questions About Sample Type

#### H3: Is it a stock sample, custom sample, pre-production sample, or handmade sample?

Explain why the sample type affects what the buyer can learn.

### H2: Questions About Customization And Packaging

#### H3: What is included in the sample?

Cover logo, label, color, packaging, manual, accessories, and what is excluded.

### H2: Questions About Fees, Lead Time, And Shipping

#### H3: What costs should be confirmed before payment?

Cover sample fee, mold fee, printing fee, shipping cost, refund policy, and lead time.

### H2: What A Sample Still Does Not Prove

Explain that a sample does not prove factory reliability, bulk consistency, payment safety, legal compliance, or final quality.

### H2: Message Template To Send The Supplier

Provide a concise buyer message that asks for sample scope, cost, timing, included/excluded items, photos/videos before shipment, and next-step quotation basis.

### H2: When To Slow Down

List warning signs around vague answers, pressure to pay, changing fees, unclear sample type, and refusal to confirm details in writing.

### H2: FAQ

Answer common questions without overclaiming.
