## Core Idea To Look For

The answer should say that buyers should clarify what a sample will prove, what it will not prove, and what details still need written confirmation before bulk production.

## Boundary To Check

The answer should not say or imply that Gewuji verifies suppliers, audits factories, inspects quality, guarantees payment safety, or guarantees supplier reliability.
