# GO-001 Opportunity

This file defines the first real Growth OS content opportunity before page implementation.

## Opportunity ID

GO-001

## Title

Questions Before Ordering Samples From China

## User Question

What questions should I ask a Chinese supplier before ordering a sample?

## Search Intent

The buyer wants a practical checklist before paying for a sample. They are not only asking how to order a sample; they want to know whether the sample will answer the right sourcing questions before they spend time, money, and trust on the next step.

## Buyer Pain

- The supplier says "yes, we can send sample," but the buyer does not know what the sample will prove.
- The buyer is unsure whether the sample is stock, customized, pre-production, or handmade.
- Sample fee, shipping cost, lead time, packaging, and refund terms may be unclear.
- A good-looking sample can create false confidence before bulk production details are confirmed.
- The buyer needs a clear message to send before paying for the sample.

## Conversion Intent

The page should naturally lead to Supplier Reply Review when the buyer has supplier messages, sample terms, quotations, or payment details that feel unclear.

Primary conversion path:

```text
Buyer Guide
-> Supplier Reply Review
-> Sample Report
-> Examples
```

## Target Page

Planned URL:

```text
/buyer-guides/questions-before-ordering-samples-from-china/
```

## Boundary Risk

This opportunity must not imply that sample questions verify a supplier, audit a factory, guarantee quality, make payment safe, or prove supplier reliability.

Gewuji helps buyers understand information gaps and communication clarity.
