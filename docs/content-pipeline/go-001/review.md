# GO-001 Human Review

This review checks whether the GO-001 draft is ready to move from content pipeline to page implementation.

## 1. Positioning Review

Status: approved.

The draft fits Gewuji's boundary:

- supplier communication clarity
- buyer questions before a sample order
- missing information and follow-up questions
- practical interpretation of supplier replies

The draft avoids prohibited positioning:

- no supplier verification claim
- no factory audit claim
- no inspection claim
- no payment protection claim
- no supplier reliability guarantee
- no quality guarantee

The strongest boundary section is `What A Sample Still Does Not Prove`, because it clearly separates sample communication from verification, audit, inspection, quality, and payment safety.

### Sections Needing Rewrite Before Page Implementation

1. Opening line:

Current:

> Before ordering a sample from China, do not only ask, "Can you send a sample?"

Issue:

- Good hook, but slightly conversational for a final Buyer Guide.

Suggested direction:

- Keep the idea, make it more direct and search-friendly.

2. Direct answer intro:

Current:

> The goal is to make the supplier explain the sample scope clearly before you pay.

Issue:

- Strong and useful, but should be softened to avoid sounding adversarial.

Suggested direction:

- Reframe as "The goal is to confirm sample scope clearly before payment."

3. `When To Slow Down` payment account bullet:

Current:

> payment account details change or are not consistent with prior communication

Issue:

- Valid, but close to payment safety territory.

Suggested direction:

- Keep it as an information-consistency signal, not as payment safety advice.

4. CTA section title:

Current:

> How Gewuji Can Help

Issue:

- Acceptable, but could be stronger as a page conversion section.

Suggested direction:

- Use a sample-specific CTA such as `Need Help Reviewing A Supplier's Sample Terms?`
