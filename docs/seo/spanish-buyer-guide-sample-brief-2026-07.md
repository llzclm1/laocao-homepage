## Page Outline

### H1

Preguntas antes de pedir muestras a China

### Intro

Explain that a sample is useful only when both buyer and supplier understand what the sample is meant to prove: product version, material, finish, packaging, customization, lead time, and what may still change before bulk production.

Tone:

- practical Latin American / Spanish small-buyer advice
- clear and concrete
- not machine-translated
- no exaggerated risk claims

### Section 1: Por qué una muestra puede causar malentendidos

Cover:

- stock sample vs similar sample vs custom sample
- sample quality may not equal bulk production quality
- packaging and labeling often change later
- quote assumptions can change after the sample

### Section 2: Preguntas clave antes de pagar la muestra

Include questions such as:

- Is this a stock sample, similar sample, or custom sample?
- Which material, finish, color, and size will the sample use?
- Will packaging match the planned bulk order?
- What parts may change before mass production?
- What information is still missing before the bulk quote is firm?

### Section 3: Señales de comunicación poco claras

Cover:

- supplier says "same as bulk" without explaining what that means
- no written confirmation of materials or packaging
- sample price is clear but bulk assumptions are vague
- payment account or company name is not explained

### Section 4: Qué puede revisar Gewuji

Explain that Gewuji can help review supplier replies, sample terms, quotations, payment details, and visible factory materials to clarify what is confirmed, what remains unclear, and what to ask next.
