# YouTube Channel Plan for Gewuji Supplier Review

This plan defines how Gewuji can turn existing buyer guides, supplier reply review content, and field materials into simple YouTube assets for overseas buyers.

## 1. Positioning

Gewuji Supplier Review explains practical supplier communication checks for overseas buyers sourcing from China.

It focuses on supplier replies, quotations, sample terms, factory videos, field materials, payment-before-order risks, and next questions to ask.

Account name:

```text
Gewuji Supplier Review
```

Subtitle:

```text
Practical checks before paying Chinese suppliers.
```

Core positioning:

```text
Practical China supplier review notes for overseas buyers before samples, deposits, tooling money, and first orders.
```

## 2. What This Channel Is Not

This channel is not:

- a factory audit channel
- a legal due diligence channel
- a quality inspection channel
- a supplier blacklist channel
- a sourcing gossip channel
- a "China factory secrets" clickbait channel
- a guarantee that a supplier is safe or reliable

## 3. Audience

The target audience includes:

- first-time buyers sourcing from China
- Amazon sellers
- Shopify sellers
- small brand owners
- ecommerce sellers
- small importers
- Kickstarter / hardware teams
- buyers preparing samples, deposits, tooling money, or first bulk orders

## 4. Content Pillars

### Supplier Reply Review

- vague replies
- missing details
- next questions

### Payment-Before-Order Checks

- sample fee
- deposit
- tooling money
- payment account match

### Factory Video / Photo Interpretation

- what a video shows
- what it does not prove
- what to ask next

### Sample vs Bulk Production

- sample purpose
- packaging
- logo
- shipping
- bulk differences

## 5. Format Strategy

The first stage only uses:

- YouTube Shorts: 30-45 seconds
- Short explainers: 3-5 minutes

Do not make:

- long podcasts
- factory tour channel
- live selling
- interview show
- vlog
- random China sourcing news

## 6. Visual Style

Use:

- simple text overlays
- generic supplier reply examples
- blurred / anonymized factory material background
- muted industrial colors
- clean captions
- no sensational thumbnails
- no fake factory footage
- no exposed private factory info

Do not show:

- customer names
- factory names
- logos
- drawings
- order numbers
- faces
- sensitive labels
- private production details

## 7. CTA Rules

Use soft CTAs:

- See the sample supplier reply review report on Gewuji.
- Compare your supplier reply against a checklist before paying.
- If you want to see how a review is structured, check the sample report.

Video description links allowed:

- `https://gewuji.dev/supplier-reply-review/`
- `https://gewuji.dev/supplier-reply-review/sample-report/`
- relevant buyer guide URL

Do not say in videos:

- Contact us now
- We guarantee safe suppliers
- We verify suppliers for you
- We can prove the factory is reliable

## 8. Publishing Rhythm

Suggested test rhythm for 8 weeks:

- 2 Shorts per week
- 1 short explainer per week

Minimum first test:

- 5 Shorts
- based on the first 5 buyer guides

## 9. Success Metrics

Track:

- views
- average view duration
- retention
- comments
- profile clicks
- website clicks
- YouTube referral traffic
- subscriber growth
- topics with higher completion rate

## 10. Relationship With Existing Content

Buyer guides are the source content.

YouTube videos are not replacements for website content.

Each video should point back to a relevant buyer guide or supplier reply review page when useful.

Priority order remains:

1. `/supplier-reply-review/`
2. `/buyer-guides/`
3. LinkedIn / Reddit / Quora external distribution
4. YouTube Shorts / explainers
5. factory-side content
