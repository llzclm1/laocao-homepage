## 1. 目标

获取：

3-5 个真实海外买家测试案例。

成功标准不是流量。

成功标准是：

- 有人提交 supplier reply。

## 3. 首次沟通模板

### English template

Hi [Name],

I am testing a small manual Supplier Reply Review format for buyers who are talking with Chinese suppliers before a sample fee, deposit, tooling payment, or first order.

It does not verify the supplier, audit the factory, inspect product quality, or guarantee any result.

The review is more practical and narrow: it looks at one supplier reply, quotation, payment message, or sample discussion and identifies:

1. what the supplier has actually confirmed
2. what is still unclear
3. what questions you should ask next
4. a short English reply you can send back

If you have a supplier reply you are unsure about, you can redact private details and send it as a test case.

No need to send sensitive payment information unless you are comfortable. Company names, personal names, and account numbers can be removed.

### Short version

Hi [Name], I am testing a small Supplier Reply Review format for buyers before sample fees or deposits. It does not verify the supplier or guarantee results. It only helps identify what is confirmed, what is unclear, and what to ask next. If you have a supplier reply you are unsure about, you can redact private details and send it as a test case.

Avoid:

- guarantee
- verified supplier
- risk-free
