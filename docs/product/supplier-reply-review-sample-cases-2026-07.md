# Supplier Reply Review 匿名案例库：2026-07

这份文档用于沉淀 Supplier Reply Review 的匿名样例，帮助后续交付时保持判断边界和报告结构一致。

## Case 1: Payment account mismatch

### Situation

买家准备支付样品费或定金。供应商聊天中使用一个公司名，报价单上是另一个公司名，收款账户又显示第三个账户名。

### Supplier message example

> Please send the deposit to this bank account. It is our finance account. The company name on Alibaba is different because we use another export company.

### What is confirmed

- 供应商已经提出付款请求。
- 供应商说明收款账户与平台显示公司名不同。
- 供应商给出的理由是使用了另一个出口公司或财务账户。

### What is unclear

- 平台公司名、报价单公司名、收款账户名之间是什么关系。
- 收款账户是否属于同一业务主体或授权收款方。
- PI、invoice、合同或订单确认中应使用哪个公司名。
- 付款后由哪个主体负责样品、生产和售后沟通。

### Possible risk signals

- company name mismatch
- payment account not clearly explained
- supplier asks for payment before written clarification
- invoice name and bank account name are not aligned

### Questions to ask next

- Can you explain the relationship between the Alibaba company name, invoice company name, and bank account name?
- Which company will be responsible for this order after payment?
- Can you issue the PI with the same company name as the payment account?
- If the payment account belongs to another company, can you provide written authorization or a clear explanation on the PI?

### Suggested buyer reply

Before I arrange payment, could you please clarify the company name details in writing?

I need to understand the relationship between the company name shown on Alibaba, the company name on the PI, and the bank account name. Please also confirm which company will be responsible for the sample order after payment.

If the payment account belongs to a different company, please explain the reason clearly on the PI or provide written confirmation.

### Boundary note

This mismatch does not prove the supplier is fake. It is an information gap before payment and should be clarified in writing.

## Case 2: Supplier quote too low

### Situation

买家收到几家供应商报价，其中一家明显低于其他报价，但解释很模糊，只说“we have best price”。

### Supplier message example

> Our price is much cheaper because we are factory direct. Quality is same, no problem. You can place order first and we can start quickly.

### What is confirmed

- 供应商报价低于买家看到的其他报价。
- 供应商声称自己是 factory direct。
- 供应商表示质量没有问题，并推动买家尽快下单。

### What is unclear

- 低价来自材料、规格、包装、产能、付款条件还是报价遗漏。
- 报价是否包含相同产品版本。
- 包装、logo、认证、测试、运费或模具费用是否另算。
- 大货是否会使用与样品相同的材料和工艺。

### Possible risk signals

- very low price with vague explanation
- supplier says quality is the same without details
- product specification is not written down
- supplier pushes order before confirming scope

### Questions to ask next

- What exact product version is included in this price?
- What material, size, finish, packaging, and logo option does this quote include?
- Are there any extra costs not included in the unit price?
- Is the bulk order made with the same material and process as the sample?
- Why is this quote lower than the usual market range?

### Suggested buyer reply

Thank you for the quotation. Before I compare it with other offers, could you confirm exactly what is included in this price?

Please specify the material, size, finish, packaging, logo option, MOQ, lead time, and any extra costs. I also need to know whether the sample and bulk order will use the same material and production process.

Once these points are clear, I can review the quote properly.

### Boundary note

A low quote is not automatically a bad sign. The issue is whether the supplier can explain what is included and what may change later.

## Case 3: Sample vs bulk uncertainty

### Situation

买家准备订样。供应商确认可以寄样，但没有说明样品证明什么，也没有说明大货和样品可能有哪些差异。

### Supplier message example

> Sample is available. You can check quality first. Bulk order will be no problem. Packaging can discuss after sample.

### What is confirmed

- 供应商可以提供样品。
- 供应商认为买家可以先看样品质量。
- 包装细节尚未确认。

### What is unclear

- 样品是否为现货样、定制样还是类似样。
- 样品的材料、颜色、尺寸、logo、包装是否等同于大货。
- 大货生产时哪些部分可能变化。
- 样品确认后，批量订单还需要重新确认哪些细节。

### Possible risk signals

- sample scope not defined
- packaging pushed to later
- bulk difference not explained
- supplier uses "no problem" without written details

### Questions to ask next

- Is this sample the exact same version as the future bulk order?
- What material, color, size, finish, and logo will the sample include?
- Will the bulk order use the same packaging as the sample?
- What details may change between sample and mass production?
- What should be confirmed again before bulk order?

### Suggested buyer reply

Before I order the sample, could you please confirm what the sample will prove?

Please confirm whether the sample will use the exact material, color, size, finish, logo, and packaging planned for the bulk order. If any part may change in bulk production, please list it clearly.

I want to avoid approving a sample that does not match the final order.

### Boundary note

A sample can reduce uncertainty, but it does not confirm every bulk production detail unless the sample scope is written clearly.

## Case 4: Factory video visibility issue

### Situation

买家要求看工厂视频。供应商发来一段录制视频，只展示 showroom、门头和几排机器，没有展示与买家产品相关的生产区域。

### Supplier message example

> This is our factory video. You can see we are real factory. Many machines and workers. If you want order, please pay sample fee first.

### What is confirmed

- 供应商提供了工厂相关视频。
- 视频中可能出现 showroom、设备或人员。
- 供应商希望买家先支付样品费。

### What is unclear

- 视频是否为当前供应商自己的现场。
- 视频是否与买家的产品品类相关。
- 供应商是否控制样品、包装、定制或关键工序。
- 是否可以进行 live video call。
- 哪些环节外包，哪些环节内部完成。

### Possible risk signals

- recorded video only
- video does not show relevant production flow
- showroom shown instead of sample room or packaging area
- supplier treats video as proof instead of context

### Questions to ask next

- Can we do a short live video call before sample payment?
- Can you show the sample room and similar products during the call?
- Can you show the packaging area or label/carton examples if relevant?
- Which production steps are done in your own facility?
- Which steps are outsourced?

### Suggested buyer reply

Thank you for sending the video. Before I pay the sample fee, could we do a short live video call?

During the call, I would like to see the sample room, similar products, packaging area if available, and the production steps related to this product category. Please also let me know which steps are handled in your own facility and which are outsourced.

This will help me understand the order setup better.

### Boundary note

Factory photos or videos are useful signals, but they are not formal audit evidence and cannot prove everything about the supplier or future order.

## Case 5: Supplier vague technical answers

### Situation

买家问到材料、尺寸公差、表面处理或定制细节时，供应商一直回复“yes, can do”，但没有提供具体参数。

### Supplier message example

> Yes, we can make it. Material is good quality. Size can be customized. Color no problem. We have done many orders before.

### What is confirmed

- 供应商声称可以定制。
- 供应商没有拒绝买家的技术要求。
- 供应商表示有相关经验。

### What is unclear

- 具体材料名称或等级。
- 尺寸范围和公差。
- 颜色、表面处理或 logo 工艺的限制。
- 定制是否需要模具费、起订量或额外周期。
- 供应商是否真正理解买家的技术要求。

### Possible risk signals

- yes to everything
- technical answer lacks numbers or specifications
- customization scope not defined
- supplier avoids detailed confirmation

### Questions to ask next

- What exact material grade will you use?
- What size tolerance can you control for this product?
- What finish or color options are available for this material?
- Does this customization require tooling cost or higher MOQ?
- Can you confirm these specifications in the quotation or PI?

### Suggested buyer reply

Thanks for confirming that customization is possible. To avoid misunderstanding, could you please reply with the exact technical details?

Please confirm the material grade, size tolerance, surface finish or color options, logo method, MOQ, tooling cost if any, and lead time impact. It would be helpful if these details can also be included in the quotation or PI.

### Boundary note

Vague technical replies do not prove the supplier is unreliable. They show that the buyer needs more specific written confirmation before sample payment or deposit.
