# Notion Public Checklist 模板

这份模板用于创建一个公开 Notion 页面，主题是样品单前的供应商沟通清单。

## 页面标题

```text
Supplier Communication Checklist Before a Sample Order
```

## Intro

```text
This checklist is for overseas buyers preparing early conversations with Chinese suppliers.

The goal is simple: ask clearer questions before a sample order, compare replies from a communication angle, and collect enough product and factory materials to understand what is being offered.
```

## Checklist

```md
## 1. Basic supplier information

- Company name in English and Chinese
- Business address
- Production or warehouse location
- Contact person's role
- Product category they actually handle

## 2. Product and material clarity

- Main material
- Size, weight, color, and finish
- Packaging method
- Customization limits
- Photos or videos related to the exact product category

## 3. Sample order questions

- Sample cost
- Sample lead time
- Shipping method
- Whether the sample uses the same material as bulk orders
- What changes before mass production

## 4. Reply quality

- Are answers specific or vague?
- Do photos match the product category?
- Does the supplier explain limits clearly?
- Are MOQ, packaging, and lead time consistent across replies?
- Can they show recent workshop, sample room, or warehouse materials?

## 5. Field materials to request

- Workshop or warehouse photos
- Product close-up photos
- Packaging photos
- Label or carton examples
- Short video from the production or sample area

## Related pages

- [practical sourcing communication guide](https://gewuji.dev/for-buyers/)
- [field materials for buyer trust](https://gewuji.dev/field-materials/)
```

## 推荐公开设置

- 页面允许公开访问。
- 不开启评论。
- 不放联系方式。
- 不写成交、询盘或供应商可靠性承诺。
