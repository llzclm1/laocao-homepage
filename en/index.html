About

Turning small ideas into visible things

After 45, I care less about how much I own and more about how much curiosity I can still keep.

I like motorcycles for the feeling of freedom, coffee for the process, photography for holding passing moments, and 3D printing for turning ideas into objects.

I also like films, music, computers, AI, and new tools because curiosity is still the best way I know to stay young.
