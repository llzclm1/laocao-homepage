About

Turning small ideas into visible things

GEWUJI is a personal product lab by Laocao. It is not a single startup pitch deck; it is a working shelf for things that are being built, tested and refined.

The projects come from everyday interests: photography, motorcycles, games, AI tools, small business workflows, information capture and the stubborn joy of making something real.

Some projects are already online. Some are early experiments. The point is to keep building, keep learning from real use, and keep a visible record of the process.

What is GEWUJI?

GEWUJI is Laocao's personal product lab. It collects small products, tools and experiments including PixRoom, Sticker Booth Lab, memories, Zhipu, Business Mini Tools, Office Survivor and Motorcycle Garage.

What is memories?

memories is a desktop inbox for capturing useful information from chats, webpages, screenshots and daily research. Free Mac and Windows builds are available.
